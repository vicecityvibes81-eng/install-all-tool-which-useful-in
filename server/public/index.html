💻 CLI Commands & Antigravity Helpers

All helper tools are installed in your shell PATH. You can run them directly in the terminal or integrate them into automated scripts.

1. Quick Lossless Trim

# vclip    [output]
vclip podcast.mp4 00:01:30 00:02:15 highlight.mp4

# Re-encode for frame-accurate cuts
vclip podcast.mp4 10 45 clip.mp4 --reencode --crf 18

2. Download Specific Clip from URL (YouTube/Twitch/TikTok)

# vdl    [output.mp4]
vdl "https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ" 00:00:15 00:00:45 rick_clip.mp4

3. Convert 16:9 Landscape to 9:16 Vertical Short

# Blurred background standard (TikTok/Reels/Shorts)
vshort clip.mp4 viral_short.mp4 --style blur

# Center crop
vshort clip.mp4 viral_short.mp4 --style crop

4. Strip Awkward Silences and Dead Air

vsilence interview.mp4 tight_interview.mp4 --threshold 4%

5. Split Video by Scene Transitions

vscenes gameplay.mp4 ./output_scenes/ --threshold 27

6. Speech-to-Text & Auto Subtitles (Whisper)

# Generate SRT subtitles
vsub clip.mp4

# Burn subtitles directly into video
vsub clip.mp4 --burn

7. Media Inspector

vinfo clip.mp4
